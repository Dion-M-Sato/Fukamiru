# Japanese prose style policy

Translation, manuscript generation and proofreading share a natural-Japanese policy.
Preserve meaning, source attribution, negation, modality, numbers and direct quotations.
Prefer finite verbs and clear actors when they are known. Avoid unsupported stock claims,
empty intensifiers, repetitive openings and unnecessary nominalizations.

Paragraph redrafting may change sentence boundaries and order inside a stable paragraph.
Keep canonical HTML structure, paragraph IDs, protected human blocks, citations and their
source anchors. Independent audits compare the exact candidate against the source before
accepting it. If a candidate loses a protected invariant or cannot be verified, retain the
source and present the unresolved issue for human review.

The surface gate reports repeated light-verb nominalization conservatively. Its purpose
is to identify generated accumulation without rejecting legitimate conceptual terms.
Restricted proofreading applies bounded edits subject to semantic and structural checks.

Assess vocabulary, collocation, syntax, reference clarity, abstraction, rhythm and academic
register separately. Use fictional examples in regression tests; automated checks establish
preservation and routing behavior, while prose quality also requires human reading.
