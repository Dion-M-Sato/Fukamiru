# Bibliography style

The renderer supports explicitly verified Japanese and English bibliographic records.
Preserve uncertainty rather than inventing missing people, editions, venues or dates.
Responsibility roles must be supported by source metadata; an authority lookup is not
substituted for an absent author or translator role.

Japanese books and journals use 『 』, while articles, poems and chapters use 「 」.
Render authors, title, translators or editors, publication place, publisher, series,
year and locator in the order appropriate to the record type. Do not display country
codes as publication places. Footnotes have their own field order. Preserve punctuation
inside titles and separate distinct references into individual canonical blocks.

The Japanese reference rules cite the relevant provisions of the published author guide:
<https://www.sjllf.org/file/1707>. Source revisions describe that external guide rather
than a local instruction date. Record types and languages without a supported style
remain reviewable, with their supplied metadata preserved.

Publisher corrections require corroborated evidence bound to the reference ID, title,
author and every supplied publication-identity field. Conflicting evidence remains for
human review. The application contains no built-in correction for a particular work.
